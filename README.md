# Celesa

Hold a key, talk, let go: clean text in your own voice appears wherever you type. Everything runs on your Mac.

**[Download Celesa 0.6.0 for Mac](https://github.com/thenaaiops-coder/celesa-releases/releases/download/v0.6.0/Celesa-0.6.0.dmg)** (macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon)

1. Open the file and drag Celesa onto Applications.
2. Open Celesa. If macOS says it "could not verify" it, click Done, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once: later updates install from inside the app.
3. Allow Microphone and Accessibility when asked. Hold Right Option and talk.

## What's new in 0.6.0

- **Speakeazy is now Celesa.** New name, new logo (the chime C) and new app icon on Mac, Windows and iPhone. Named after the celesta, a keyboard that sounds like bells: Celesa is the keyboard with a voice
- Updating from 0.5.x: the app installs itself as Celesa, moves the old copy to the Trash and keeps your settings, dictionary, snippets, history and downloaded models. Allow Microphone and Accessibility once more, because the app has a new identity (app.celesa.mac)
- Downloads move to https://github.com/thenaaiops-coder/celesa-releases. The old download page points there
- New listening animation, Trail: a fine glowing line in blue, violet, pink and orange that follows your voice, with fading echoes of the last half second. It sweeps colour while Celesa writes and ends with a check and "Pasted". Replaces the robot (Mac, Windows, iPhone)

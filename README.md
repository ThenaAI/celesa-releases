# Celesa

Hold a key, talk, let go: clean text in your own voice appears wherever you type. Everything runs on your Mac.

**[Download Celesa 0.8.0 for Mac](https://github.com/ThenaAI/celesa-releases/releases/download/v0.8.0/Celesa-0.8.0.dmg)** (macOS 14 or later, Apple Silicon)

1. Open the file and drag Celesa onto Applications.
2. Open Celesa. If macOS says it "could not verify" it, click Done, then go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and click **Open Anyway**. You only do this once: later updates install from inside the app.
3. Allow Microphone and Accessibility when asked. Hold Right Option and talk.

## What's new in 0.8.0

- **Guided first-run setup** (Mac, Windows, iPhone): one window walks through every permission with pictures of what to click, sets up your voice and lets you try a first sentence. No trips to Settings. People updating who are already set up don't see it
- **Help train Silent mode** (Mac): in the menu. Agree, rest your face for 3 seconds, then read short sentences on camera. Everything stays on your Mac, and "Delete all recordings" removes it all
- **Learn my lips while I dictate** (Mac, Settings > Silent mode, off by default): the camera records while you hold the hotkey so Silent mode learns how you speak. Clips stay on your Mac
- Settings has a Silent mode section on Mac and Windows. Its switch unlocks when the lip-reading model ships
